# AtlasBible Privacy Policy

**Effective November 9, 2026**

Namib publishes AtlasBible: Bible Maps & Study for Android (`com.namib.atlasbible`). This policy explains how the app handles information and what happens when you visit this website or contact us.

## What AtlasBible stores

AtlasBible works offline. The Bible text, maps, and reference material ship with the app. Your device processes Bible searches and stores your reading position, display preferences, bookmarks, verse highlights, and notes so you can pick up where you left off.

AtlasBible has no account system or cloud sync operated by Namib. Namib does not receive your Bible searches, bookmarks, highlights, or notes. The map shows biblical places and does not use your device's precise location.

## Advertising and privacy choices

Versions with advertising may use Google AdMob to display a banner on **Person details**, **Place details**, and **Browse all journeys** when online. In the journey catalog, banners appear only when **All entries** is selected and no search filter is active. AtlasBible does not include your Bible search terms, reading position, or saved study in ad requests.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, which can indicate approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, including your Android advertising ID if available. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention and encrypts it in transit. Google's privacy policy describes how Google handles and retains this information. [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether privacy choices are required. When available, you can review them under **Menu → Reading settings → Ad privacy choices**. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Reading and study features work offline.

## Backups and files you choose

Android may back up or transfer your app preferences and saved study as part of its system backup and device transfer features. Availability and storage depend on your Android settings and backup provider. Manage system backups through Android or your provider.

If you export study data, AtlasBible writes your bookmarks, highlights, and notes to a readable JSON file at the location you select in Android's file picker. If you select a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the file under its own privacy terms. Exported files are not encrypted by AtlasBible. When you restore a backup, AtlasBible reads the file you select and imports the study data after you confirm.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

AtlasBible keeps your saved study and settings in the app's private storage on your device. Use **Bookmarks** or **Highlights & notes** to remove individual entries. To remove the app's local data, clear AtlasBible's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. If you also want to remove exported files or system backups, manage those copies in the location or service where they were saved.

## Website, external links, and support

When you open a source link or this policy from the app, your browser handles the visit and the destination's privacy policy applies. GitHub Pages hosts this policy and logs visitor IP addresses for security. [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

If you email Namib, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond and keep it as needed to handle your inquiry. You can ask us to delete information you sent. Please avoid attaching private study notes unless they are necessary to explain your request.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Namib at [pat@namibapp.com](mailto:pat@namibapp.com). If this policy changes, we will post the updated text and effective date on this page.
